Cookie Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

This policy explains how Closelix uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. For more information about how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable core functionality of our website
  • Understand how visitors use our site through analytics
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Deliver relevant marketing and advertising
  • Secure our website and prevent fraud

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. They enable core features like page navigation, secure areas access, and form submissions. You cannot opt out of essential cookies without impacting website functionality.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains user session state Session
csrf_token Security protection against cross-site request forgery Session
consent_banner Remembers cookie consent preferences 1 year

3.2 Performance Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website. They help us understand which pages are most visited, how visitors navigate the site, and identify any errors users encounter.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics: distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_gid Google Analytics: distinguishes unique users per session 24 hours
_gat Google Analytics: throttles request rate 1 minute

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember your preferences and choices, such as language settings and display preferences. They provide a more personalized experience.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration

timezone Remembers your timezone for local time display 1 year
recent_views Remembers recently viewed pages 30 days

3.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your browsing activity across websites to deliver targeted advertising. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_fbp Facebook Pixel: tracks conversions and delivers targeted ads 3 months

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks website usage and performance
  • Google Ads: Delivers targeted advertising based on your activity
  • Facebook Pixel: Tracks conversions and enables remarketing
  • LinkedIn Ads: Delivers targeted advertising on LinkedIn

5. How to Manage Cookies

5.1 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block cookies from all or specific websites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close your browser

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5.2 Opt-Out Links

You can opt out of specific third-party cookies:

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out
  • Facebook Ads Preferences
  • LinkedIn Ads Settings

5.3 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner. You can choose to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customize your preferences. Your preferences are stored for one year.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some features of our website may not work properly:

  • Some pages may not load correctly
  • You may need to re-enter login information on each visit
  • Your preferences will not be saved
  • Analytics will not track your activity
